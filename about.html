关于我们 / About Us

公司简介 / Company Profile

金实公司是一家成熟的纳米级陶瓷粉末出口公司，以我们在福建省漳州市的制造工厂为基础，专注于锆基陶瓷原材料的生产和出口。

Jinshi is a mature nano-ceramic powder export company, based on our manufacturing plant in Zhangzhou City, Fujian Province, focusing on the production and export of zirconium-based ceramic raw materials.

我们专业生产全系列的硅酸锆、二氧化锆、电熔氧化锆、复合氧化锆和研磨介质等锆产品。

We specialize in producing a full range of zirconium products including zircon, zirconia, fused zirconia, composite zirconia and grinding media.

从 30 年前作为锆供应商开始，已经发展成为一家在中国拥有5个销售办事处并出口到印度地区、东南亚、欧洲的全面生产工厂。

Starting as a zirconium supplier 30 years ago, we have grown into a comprehensive manufacturing plant with 5 sales offices in China and exports to India, Southeast Asia, and Europe.

企业文化 / Corporate Culture

我们一向注重企业文化建设，在保持员工个性的同时力求营造一种企业与员工互为依托的和谐氛围。

We always focus on corporate culture development, striving to create a harmonious atmosphere where the company and employees support each other while maintaining individual personalities.

我们热切关注并积极参与各种社会公益活动，奉献爱心、回报社会。

We actively participate in various social welfare activities, contributing to society with love and dedication.
